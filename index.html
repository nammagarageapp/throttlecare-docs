Complete SaaS Platform & Mobile App Guide

ThrottleCare (Namma Garage) is an enterprise-grade independent garage management platform connecting Garage Owners, Mechanics, Customers, and Super Administrators in real time with Supabase cloud sync. Click on any section below to expand and explore step-by-step use cases.

🛠️ Setting Up Your Garage Code & Profile

When launching the app for the first time, register as an Admin. Enter your garage name, phone number, operating city, and create a unique Garage Code (e.g. `NG-9812`). This code securely scopes all your data in Supabase.

👷 Adding Mechanics & Staff

Go to the People tab from the bottom navigation. Tap + Add Staff, enter the mechanic's name, email, PIN, and role (e.g., Senior Technician, Supervisor). Mechanics are added instantly to your staff roster and can log in immediately using their PIN.

🚗 Creating Service Job Cards

Navigate to the Jobs tab, tap + New Job Card. Fill in customer details, vehicle plate number, service type, odometer reading, and attach walkaround inspection photos using the zero-permission Android Photo Picker. Assign tasks directly to active mechanics.

📦 Inventory & Low Stock Alerts

Manage spare parts, lubricants, and tools in the Inventory tab. Set minimum quantity thresholds. Items falling below the threshold automatically trigger low-stock alerts for quick procurement ordering.

💳 Billing, UPI QR & Revenue

In the Bills tab, generate professional invoices, upload your workshop UPI VPA QR code for direct customer scanning, and track monthly collected revenue vs. outstanding bills.

🔑 Quick PIN Authentication

Mechanics select the Labour role tab on the login screen, enter their registered email and 4–6 digit PIN, or use biometric quick unlock.

🟢 Active Duty & Leave Status

Easily toggle duty status (On Duty, On Leave with reasons like Sick Leave / Family Event, or Off Duty) so supervisors and admins know who is available in the workshop bays.

📋 Managing Assigned Jobs & WhatsApp Sharing

View assigned repair jobs, update repair progress percentages, add part photos, and instantly share live job progress reports via WhatsApp with customers with one tap.

📊 Live Vehicle Health & Progress

Customers log in with their vehicle plate number to track real-time repair progress, view odometer readings, inspect walkaround photos, and see estimated pick-up readiness.

📖 Digital Service Logbook

Access historical maintenance records, oil change dates, battery/tyre replacements, and uploaded service receipts in one organized digital logbook.

🏷️ Member Perks & SOS

Access workshop member offers, discounts on synthetic oil changes, and one-tap roadside assistance WhatsApp SOS.

🏢 Multi-Garage Monitoring

Platform maintainers and super admins (`nammagarageapp@gmail.com`) can view all registered independent garages, active staff counts, and platform-wide metrics from a centralized dashboard.

💳 Subscriptions & Plan Tiers

Manage garage subscription tiers (Free, Pro, Enterprise), bay capacities, and renewal cycles.

🎫 Support Tickets & Telemetry

Review user-submitted support tickets, bug logs, and real-time app observability telemetry.

🛡️ Biometric Quick Unlock

Leverages Android BiometricPrompt and AES-256-GCM encryption backed by the hardware-backed Android Keystore for secure, password-less login.

🗑️ Permanent Account & Data Deletion

Compliant with Google Play Data Safety policies. Users can permanently wipe their local credentials, biometrics, and cloud-synced profile data instantly from Security Settings.

🚀 How to Host This on GitHub Pages

  1. Create a public repository on GitHub (e.g. `throttlecare-brochure`).
  2. Upload brochure.html and **rename it to index.html**.
  3. Go to your repository **Settings** → **Pages**.
  4. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch** and choose `main` / `root`.
  5. Click **Save**, and within 60 seconds your interactive documentation brochure will be live on GitHub Pages!
