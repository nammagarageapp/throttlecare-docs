Complete SaaS Platform Guide & Privacy Policy

ThrottleCare (Namma Garage) is an enterprise-grade independent garage management platform connecting Garage Owners, Mechanics, Customers, and Super Administrators in real time with Supabase cloud sync. Explore the interactive guide below or review our official Privacy Policy.

📖 Interactive Platform User Guide

🛠️ Setting Up Your Garage Code & Profile

When launching the app for the first time, register as an Admin. Enter your garage name, phone number, operating city, and create a unique Garage Code (e.g. `NG-9812`). This code securely scopes all your data in Supabase.

👷 Adding Mechanics & Staff

Go to the People tab from the bottom navigation. Tap + Add Staff, enter the mechanic's name, email, PIN, and role. Mechanics are added instantly to your staff roster and can log in immediately using their PIN.

🚗 Creating Service Job Cards

Navigate to the Jobs tab, tap + New Job Card. Fill in customer details, vehicle plate number, service type, odometer reading, and attach walkaround inspection photos using the zero-permission Android Photo Picker.

📦 Inventory & Low Stock Alerts

Manage spare parts, lubricants, and tools in the Inventory tab. Set minimum quantity thresholds to trigger automated low-stock alerts.

💳 Billing, UPI QR & Revenue

In the Bills tab, generate professional invoices, upload your workshop UPI VPA QR code for direct customer scanning, and track monthly revenue.

🔑 Quick PIN Authentication

Mechanics select the Labour role tab on the login screen, enter their registered email and 4–6 digit PIN, or use biometric quick unlock.

🟢 Active Duty & Leave Status

Easily toggle duty status (On Duty, On Leave with reasons like Sick Leave / Family Event, or Off Duty) so supervisors know bay availability.

📋 Managing Assigned Jobs & WhatsApp Sharing

View assigned repair jobs, update repair progress percentages, add part photos, and instantly share live job progress reports via WhatsApp with customers.

📊 Live Vehicle Health & Progress

Customers log in with their vehicle plate number to track real-time repair progress, view odometer readings, inspect walkaround photos, and see pick-up readiness.

📖 Digital Service Logbook

Access historical maintenance records, oil change dates, battery/tyre replacements, and uploaded service receipts in one organized digital logbook.

🏷️ Member Perks & SOS

Access workshop member offers, discounts on synthetic oil changes, and one-tap roadside assistance WhatsApp SOS.

🏢 Multi-Garage Monitoring

Platform maintainers and super admins (`nammagarageapp@gmail.com`) can view all registered independent garages, active staff counts, and platform-wide metrics.

💳 Subscriptions & Plan Tiers

Manage garage subscription tiers (Starter, Pro, Enterprise), bay capacities, and renewal cycles.

🎫 Support Tickets & Telemetry

Review user-submitted support tickets, bug logs, and real-time app observability telemetry.

Official Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Last updated: November 2026 • ThrottleCare (Namma Garage Platform)

1. Introduction & Overview

ThrottleCare ("Namma Garage") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and operational data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and SaaS platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Data: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and encrypted security PINs/passwords required for role authentication (Admin, Labour, Customer, Super Admin).
  • Workshop & Vehicle Records: Independent garage names, workshop codes, vehicle registration plate numbers, model details, service job cards, odometer readings, and repair history.
  • Media Attachments: Walkaround inspection photos, service bill receipts, and UPI QR code screenshots captured via the zero-permission Android Photo Picker (`PickVisualMedia`) or camera when uploading service records.
  • Biometric Data: Local device biometric credentials (fingerprint/face unlock) are processed entirely via the hardware-backed Android Keystore and BiometricPrompt; biometric templates never leave your device and are never stored on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly to provide, maintain, and improve our services, including:

  • Operating multi-tenant garage workflows, repair job card tracking, and staff duty management.
  • Synchronizing workshop records in real time over secure TLS/HTTPS connections with Supabase cloud infrastructure.
  • Sending job progress updates and invoices via WhatsApp or push notifications.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between the ThrottleCare app and Supabase cloud database is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS. Database access is strictly isolated per garage code (`garage_code`) via Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain full control over your data. You may permanently delete your account credentials, local biometric profiles, and cloud-synced workshop records at any time directly within the app under Security & Account Settings in the top bar. Upon requesting deletion, all associated user-owned records are wiped from active cloud storage.
